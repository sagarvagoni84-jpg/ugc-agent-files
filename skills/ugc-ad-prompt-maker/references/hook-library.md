# Hook library for 20-second UGC ads (US/UK)

A hook is a VISUAL + a LINE that lands in the first 2 seconds and makes the right buyer feel "that's me". It must work with sound off (what is on screen + the first caption) and sound on (4-7 spoken words). The hook is never the brand name and never a greeting.

## 1. The 12 hook formulas that keep working

| # | Formula | Spoken example (4-7 words) | Opening visual |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Mid-action reveal** | "Okay watch this one pass." | Hand already dragging the tool across the couch; hair collecting |
| 2 | **Specific frustration** | "Out of lint roller sheets. Again." | Empty roller held up, couch covered in hair behind it |
| 3 | **Quiet confession** | "I almost returned this." | Creator looks at product in hand, slight head tilt |
| 4 | **Unexpected result** | "Did not expect it to do that." | Close insert of the result mid-happening |
| 5 | **Rule-break / contrarian** | "Stop buying the sticky ones." | Points to old solution in the bin, product in the other hand |
| 6 | **Before you buy** | "Before you buy this, one thing." | Product held to camera, serious-ish face |
| 7 | **POV situation** | "Guests in ten minutes. Couch." | Phone propped, creator walks in with the tool |
| 8 | **Question the viewer is already asking** | "Does it actually work on velvet?" | Hand hovering over velvet cushion |
| 9 | **Tiny detail obsession** | "It's this little hinge for me." | Macro-ish insert of the mechanism |
| 10 | **Satisfying sound** | (no words, 1.5s) then "Hear that?" | Cap click / zipper / pour in close-up |
| 11 | **Old way vs this** | "Sheets, batteries... or this." | Two items set down, product placed last |
| 12 | **Honest limit** | "It's not magic. But look." | Product used on a tough spot |

Pick the formula that matches the awareness stage: unaware -> 7 or 1; problem-aware -> 2, 5, 11; solution-aware -> 8, 11, 12; product-aware -> 6, 9, 3; most-aware -> 1 + a verified offer in the CTA.

## 2. Writing rules

- 4-7 words spoken. The sentence must be sayable in one breath.
- Contractions and fragments are good: "Out of sheets. Again."
- Present tense, happening now.
- One idea. Do not explain the product in the hook.
- The visual must already show the product, the problem, or the result. Frame 1 is never a face saying hello.
- Caption for sound-off viewers: 3-6 words, same idea, different wording is fine ("lint roller empty... again").
- US tone: direct, "okay so", "honestly", "watch this". UK tone: drier, "right", "to be fair", "this is quietly brilliant" (do not overuse "brilliant").

## 3. Banned openers (they signal "ad" and get skipped)

"Hey guys", "Hi everyone", "Are you tired of...", "You won't believe...", "This changed my life", "Stop scrolling", "Everyone needs this", "Game changer", "Let me show you something amazing", "Introducing...", "As a busy mom...", "I've been using this for 3 months" (fabricated history), "Run, don't walk", "TikTok made me buy it" (overused), the brand name as the first words.

## 4. Scoring rubric (score each of your 5 hooks 1-5)

- **Recognition**: does the buyer see their own situation in frame 1?
- **Curiosity gap**: is there a reason to watch the next 3 seconds?
- **Product visible or implied** in the first 2 seconds?
- **Sayable** in one breath at a natural pace?
- **Renderable**: simple action, one hand, no text, no complex physics?
- **Policy-clean**: no claim, no brand, no body focus?

Pick the highest total; break ties toward the simpler visual.

## 5. Hook -> next beat bridge

The hook must hand off cleanly to 2.0-6.0s. Write the bridge line immediately: hook "Out of lint roller sheets. Again." -> bridge "So I tried the reusable one." The bridge names the product category, not the benefit pile.

## 6. Category starters (adapt, never copy verbatim)

- **Skincare**: "This is the pea-sized rule." / "Why it pills under makeup." / "Watch it sink in." (then show absorption on the back of the hand)
- **Kitchen gadget**: "One tomato. One pass." / "This lives in the drawer now." / "Hear that seal?"
- **Cleaning**: "Guests in ten. Couch." / "Stop buying the sticky ones."
- **Pet**: "Golden retriever owners, look." / "One pass, no sheets."
- **Fashion / footwear**: "Walked in these all day." (only if comfort is page-supported; otherwise "Here's how they lace.") / "Mirror check, no filter."
- **Tech**: "The button nobody mentions." / "It charges... like this."
- **Baby (parent creator)**: "The 3 a.m. version of this." / "One-hand open. That's the point."
- **Fitness**: "Strap. Clip. Done." / "The adjustment is the feature."
- **Food / drink**: "Shake, not stir. Watch." / "First sip, no sugar hit." (only if page-supported)
